Risk Management

Know exactly
how much to risk.

B&S calculates your optimal risk percentage dynamically. Win streaks step your risk up. A loss steps it back down. Two consecutive losses? The app tells you to stop, rest, and come back tomorrow.

  • Starts at 0.5% or 0.75% of balance
  • Auto-steps +0.25% per win cycle
  • Hard cap at 3.0% — always protected
  • Consecutive loss warning system
